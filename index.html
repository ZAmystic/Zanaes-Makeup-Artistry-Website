Zanae, in the studio

Hi, I'm Zanae.

I trained in Cape Town and spent four years assisting on fashion sets before starting my own studio. These days most of my time goes to brides and to editorial work for local photographers — two jobs that ask for opposite skills, and I like switching between them.

My approach stays the same either way: match the makeup to the skin and the light it'll be seen in, not to a trend. That's why the bride I did makeup for in 2019 still recognises herself in her photos.
